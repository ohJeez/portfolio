WiFi-Based Human Fall Detection

A lightweight CNN that detects falls from ESP32 WiFi Channel State Information (52 subcarriers × 500 time steps), trained on 4,380 samples with a stratified 70/15/15 split and class-weighted loss. The decision threshold (0.26) was tuned on the validation set.

92.1%recall
